Verifast Agentic AI Product

Agentic AI sales platform for 600+ e-commerce clients

Owned platform architecture from intent detection through guardrails — and fixed a production grounding failure during high-traffic flash sales.

  • Problem
  • Approach
  • Role
  • Outcome
  • Learnings

Problem

E-commerce merchants needed a sales assistant that could answer product, price, and stock questions reliably at scale — without inventing facts or drifting from live catalogue reality.

Under flash-sale load, the assistant returned stale prices and stock. Merchants and shoppers saw wrong answers exactly when accuracy mattered most.

Approach

  • Designed the agent stack around intent detection, tool/action orchestration, semantic retrieval, and business-rule guardrails.
  • Built an automated pre-go-live evaluation set so data-quality issues were caught before client launch — replacing manual catching.
  • Shipped new capabilities via sandbox testing → edge-case breaking → narrow rollout to 1–2 clients before wide release.
  • For the flash-sale incident: traced root cause to batch sync refresh interval mismatch vs real-time catalogue changes; shipped immediate mitigation plus a longer-term grounding/pipeline alignment fix.

Role

Agentic AI Product — owned the case-study architecture end-to-end and drove production diagnosis when grounding drifted from live data.

Outcome

  • Platform serving 600+ e-commerce clients with a consistent agentic sales assistant.
  • Flash-sale stale-grounding failure diagnosed in production and remediated (short-term + structural).
  • Pre-go-live eval set reduced reliance on manual data-quality catching before client launches.

Learnings

  • Grounding freshness must match the cadence of the source of truth — batch sync is a product decision, not only an infra detail.
  • Ship the mitigation that stops bleeding, then the structural fix that prevents recurrence.
  • Narrow client rollout is the cheapest way to learn where agents break before a wide release.
