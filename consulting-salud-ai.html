Salud.ai Durham MBA

US-market freemium health app sprint

Co-founded a Durham MBA consultancy sprint — GTM, AI/data strategy, KPIs, and a 15-minute client pitch that had to survive two very different audiences.

  • Problem
  • Approach
  • Role
  • Outcome
  • Learnings

Problem

Salud.ai needed a clear US freemium health-app path: go-to-market, data/AI choices, and measurable success signals — packaged for a short client presentation.

The final readout had to work for both an academic supervisor and company officials with very different attention spans.

Approach

  • Split delivery into Pod A (GTM) and Pod B (data/AI strategy).
  • Built a KPI framework, survey instruments, and Jira tickets so recommendations were executable, not only conceptual.
  • Prepared a 15-minute client presentation with a full script.
  • When the business audience disengaged from deep frameworks, cut short analysis and jumped to recommendations — keeping the client side engaged.

Role

Co-founder of the consultancy sprint; led AI & data strategy across GTM and data/AI pods, KPIs, research instruments, and delivery tickets.

Outcome

  • Integrated GTM + AI/data recommendation set for a US freemium health app.
  • Client-ready KPI framework and actionable backlog (surveys + Jira).
  • Live presentation adapted mid-stream without losing the business audience.

Learnings

  • Consulting decks must have a “skip to recommendation” path for time-poor executives.
  • Pods work when interfaces (KPIs, tickets) are shared — not when workstreams stay siloed.
  • Reading the room is a delivery skill, not an afterthought.
