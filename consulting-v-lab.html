V-Lab Strategic Consulting

Agentic AI use cases & market entry advisory

From solution architecture to market positioning — India, China, and Australia for wind-energy VR training, plus six agentic AI service concepts on digital shadow data.

Problem

V-Lab needed credible market-entry advice for wind-energy VR training across India, China, and Australia — and a clear set of agentic AI service concepts grounded in digital shadow data, not generic AI hype.

Market-sizing claims had to be rebuilt from first principles (TAM/SAM/SOM), not copied from weak secondary estimates.

Approach

  • Mapped agentic AI use cases from solution architecture through market positioning.
  • Built and corrected TAM/SAM/SOM from first principles; produced multi-country reports and an Australia Excel model.
  • Defined six agentic AI service concepts on digital shadow data.
  • Created a cross-document positioning framework so architecture, market, and offer stories stayed consistent.

Role

Strategic consulting & advisory contributor — market entry analysis, sizing models, and agentic AI offer design.

Outcome

  • Country-ready market entry materials for India, China, and Australia.
  • Defensible TAM/SAM/SOM models (including Australia Excel).
  • Six positioned agentic AI service concepts + shared positioning framework.

Learnings

  • Market sizing is a product of assumptions — rebuild them when secondary numbers don’t survive scrutiny.
  • Agentic AI offers need architecture → positioning continuity, or sales and delivery diverge.
  • Multi-country work needs one shared frame; otherwise each report becomes a one-off.
