BP Board Strategy Case

Asset divestment & AI transformation strategy

Board-facing package covering divestment choices, integrated energy for data centres, a four-pillar AI transformation agenda, and a geopolitical intelligence recommendation.

  • Problem
  • Approach
  • Role
  • Outcome
  • Learnings

Problem

The board needed a coherent view of portfolio moves (asset divestment), how BP assets could serve data-centre energy demand, how AI should reshape the enterprise, and how geopolitics should inform decisions — without drowning in disconnected slideware.

Approach

  • Asset divestment analysis as a decision frame, not a single binary recommendation.
  • IEMS for data centres linking Lightsource BP, gas, and Castrol cooling into one energy narrative.
  • AI transformation strategy organised into four pillars.
  • Geopolitical Intelligence Unit recommendation as a standing capability, not a one-off brief.
  • Deliverables layered for board use: PowerPoint, Excel financial models, Word appendices, and boardroom speech notes.

Role

Durham MBA board strategy case contributor — analysis, modelling, and board-ready storytelling across strategy, energy systems, and AI.

Outcome

  • Integrated board pack: narrative deck + financial models + appendices + speech notes.
  • Clear through-line from portfolio choices → data-centre energy opportunity → AI agenda → geopolitical sensing.

Learnings

  • Board work is multi-artefact: the speech notes matter as much as the model.
  • Energy + AI stories land when they share one decision spine instead of parallel themes.
  • Geopolitics belongs in the operating model (a unit), not only in the appendix.
